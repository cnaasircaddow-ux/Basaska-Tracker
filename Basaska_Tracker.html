Hubi taargada

App-kan sawirka si toos ah ugama akhriyo taargada. Sawir qaad kadib lambarka gacanta ku qor.

Diiwaanka basaska

Weli bas lama diiwaangelin.

Xogtu waxay ku kaydsan tahay telefoonkan/browser-kan. Ha nadiifin xogta browser-ka haddii aad rabto inaad ilaaliso diiwaanka; samee nuqul kayd ah mararka qaarkood.

Waxa app-ku qabanayo

Taargo cusub marka ugu horreysa la geliyo wuxuu ku daraa bas cusub. Marka taargo hore la geliyo mar kale, tirada maritaannada ayaa kordhaysa. Tani ma aha tirin otomaatig ah oo waddada ka dhacaysa; qof ayaa sawiraya oo gelinaya taargada.

Si app-ka loogu rakibo sida app Android ah, faylasha waa in lagu daabaco bog HTTPS ah, kadib Chrome laga doorto “Install app” ama “Add to Home screen”.
